Talk now, tired later

In one study, 74 students answered a text four times a day for four days: how tired are you now, and how sociable were you in the last hour? Being more sociable than your own usual went with better mood at the time and with more tiredness at the next check-in, 2 to 3 hours later (Leikas, 2020).

How strong is this?

  • Early: one correlational study, replicating one smaller study. It cannot show that talking causes tiredness.
  • 74 University of Helsinki students, 58 of them women; sociability was one question, "sociable".
  • The link held when time of day, earlier tiredness, work or study and simply meeting people were counted, and it did not vary between people.
  • No study measured a trade show floor. Our reading, untested: after a sociable morning, take a quiet break before the afternoon.

The estimates are averages across people, each measured against that person's own usual. Nothing you move here is stored or sent.
