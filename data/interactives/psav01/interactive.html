What the sources say

The frame pulls the line (strong): most people set the line toward a tilted frame. In a headset test with a square tilted 18 degrees, the pull was under 1 degree in 33 young men and about 1.5 degrees in 27 adults aged 50 and over (Abdul Razzak et al., 2020).

The pull depends on the tilt and on age (moderate): in virtual reality at 19 frame tilts, the largest age gap came at 15 degrees: a median 7.5 degrees in 50 older adults, 2 in 39 young adults (Adamski et al., 2026).

In the dark (strong): healthy people set a line within about 2.5 degrees of vertical; after one-sided inner-ear damage, average tilts reach about 13 degrees (Dieterich and Brandt, 2019).

This is a toy, not a test: a screen, a room and your phone's own edges all give vertical cues the lab removed. Nothing you do here is stored or sent. Education, not medical advice.
