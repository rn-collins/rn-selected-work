What the sources say

Length (strong): four times the length, twice the period; the period grows as the square root of length (Galilei, 1638).

Swing width (Galileo's claim fails for wide swings): Galileo wrote that swings of ninety down to four degrees take the same time. The exact period is longer for every swing, by about 18% at 90 degrees; the simple rule is within 0.1% only below about 7 degrees (Lima and Arun, 2006). The numbers here are computed from the exact period, in your browser.

The lamp (weak, a tradition): the story comes from Viviani in 1654; the lamp in Pisa's nave today was hung after Galileo's time, and an older lamp is in the Camposanto (Museo Galileo).
