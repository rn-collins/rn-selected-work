Where these numbers come from

Checked against measured tables (strong): this uses the standard sunrise formula with the Sun 0.833 degrees below the horizon at rise and set. It gives Pisa 8 h 55 min and a 22.8 degree noon Sun on Dec 21, 2026, and Honolulu 10 h 50 min and 45.3 degrees, the same as the US Naval Observatory's tables (queried Nov 7, 2026). Hills, buildings and weather are not included.

Why it matters (established): daylight reaching the retina is the main time signal for the brain's clock, the suprachiasmatic nuclei; morning light advances the clock and evening light delays it (Blume, Garbazza and Spitschan, 2019).
