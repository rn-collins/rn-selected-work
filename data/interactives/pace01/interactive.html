Drag the pace: twenty birthdays at different speeds

In New Zealand's Dunedin Study, members born the same year aged at different speeds. Over two decades the slowest gained about 0.40 years of biological change per calendar year, the typical member 1.0 and the fastest 2.44 (Belsky et al., 2022). Move the slider to see what twenty calendar years would mean at each pace.

20 calendar years at 1.00: about 20.0 years of body change.

Scale: the ribbon runs from 0 to 48.8 biological years (2.44 times 20). Dashed line: 20 years, the calendar.

Strength of the evidence. Strong: the range was measured in one cohort with 19 biomarkers at ages 26, 32, 38 and 45 (Belsky et al., 2022). Limited: that cohort is modest in size and from a single country (Belsky et al., 2022). This page shows group findings; it does not estimate anyone's own pace, and no test is recommended.

Small and varied: in a two-year randomized trial, eating less slowed a blood measure of this pace by about 2 to 3%, and responses varied from person to person (Waziry et al., 2023).

Education, not medical advice.
