Illustration made for this page: a textbook learning rule (prediction error = reward minus expectation; the expectation moves a little toward each outcome). It is not a recording from a brain or an app.

STRONG Most dopamine neurons in the midbrain of humans, monkeys and rodents signal a reward prediction error: above baseline when a reward beats the prediction, at baseline when it matches, below baseline when an expected reward is missing (Schultz, 2016).

EARLY Endless scroll pays off only now and then, which one theoretical analysis links to intermittent reward (Montag et al., 2019). A systematic test would need app data that usually only the companies hold.

STRONG that the average link is small: across 355,358 teenagers, digital technology use explained at most 0.4% of differences in wellbeing (Orben & Przybylski, 2019). STILL DEBATED what it means for particular platforms and groups.
