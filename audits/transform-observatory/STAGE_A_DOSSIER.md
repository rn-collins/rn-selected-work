## 2. Executive verdict

The artifact demonstrates unusually ambitious policy taxonomy, cross-domain synthesis and information architecture. Eight substantial routes connect corporate capture, IP, Indigenous knowledge, equity, development, commercialization and Transform research into one interpretive framework.

It is not yet a trustworthy “living intelligence platform.” Records are statically rendered; automation, monitoring, alerts, weekly digest and update operations are not evidenced. Some claims are high-risk or wrong, most notably an apparent false statement that DEA proposed moving psilocybin to Schedule III in 2024. Scores and editorial judgments lack transparent provenance.

The largest brand problem is affiliation ambiguity. The site looks and speaks like a Transform product, while a buried page describes it as a deliverable being sent to Steve Rolles and claims potential project opportunities. Portfolio copy says it is independent/not endorsed, but the site does not state that prominently. Retain P only with a global independence disclosure, claim-level verification, operational truthfulness and privacy/accessibility remediation.

## 7. Copy and information architecture

Distinguish “Transform source framework,” “RN interpretation,” “verified event,” “legal status,” “score,” “open question” and “forecast.” Add a visible global independence banner, per-record source/date/reviewer, update/correction log, scoring rationale and uncertainty. Replace categorical rhetoric and inside-relationship voice. Fix tab canonicals and create stable route-specific metadata.

## 8. Evidence, data quality and currentness

Institute claim-level primary sourcing, link checking, jurisdiction/effective-date fields, archived copies, status taxonomy, last verified/by whom, second review and correction history. Freeze/remove the psilocybin Schedule III item immediately. Reverify all headline statistics, quotations, patents, FDA/DEA/legal status, Nagoya/FPIC applications, equity program outcomes, actor relationships and Transform implementation claims.

No score should be presented as objective until criteria, weights, evidence, reviewer judgment, uncertainty, version and challenge process are visible. “Sweet Spot” is a normative framework, not an empirically self-executing truth label.

## 9. Drug policy, health, legal and regulatory boundary

The site is informational, not legal/medical/policy advice. It must separate enacted law, proposed rule/bill, petition, litigation, guidance, administrative implementation and editorial forecast. Drug scheduling, FDA approval, patent scope, international treaty, Indigenous law/rights, environmental rules and state programs require qualified jurisdiction-specific review. Policy benefits/harms are contested and distributional; avoid presenting a single framework’s preferred market design as universal consensus.

## 10. Privacy and security

Before alerts: publish privacy notice, consent, controller/contact, processors, security, purpose, retention/deletion, unsubscribe and sensitive-interest handling. Protect any seed/admin endpoint with authentication, authorization, CSRF/method restrictions, schema validation, rate limiting, audit logging and separation from production consumers. Avoid collecting unnecessary policy-interest profiles. Publish editorial/conflicts/corrections terms.

## 11. Accessibility and responsive behavior

Add skip navigation, robust landmarks, larger text, contrast fixes, status labels independent of color, keyboard-visible focus, accessible alert feedback/errors and mobile alternatives to wide tab navigation. Test 320px, 200/400% zoom, reduced motion, keyboard, screen reader and long citations. The responsive crop criterion is inapplicable currently because no images ship.

## 12. Brand, affiliation, portrait and authorship

The Transform name dominates product identity while RN authorship is relegated to footer/contact. Add a top-level independence disclosure on every route and clarify that framework ownership belongs to Transform while all selection, interpretation and scores are RN’s. Do not disclose or characterize private communications without permission.

No RN portrait, author image, alt text or OG image ships. Portrait use is not strategically needed across monitoring tabs and could worsen implied authority. If used for transparent authorship, place one verified portrait only in a home-page independent-builder/About block immediately before footer/contact, sourced locally with documented rights, descriptive alt, intrinsic dimensions, optimized format and tested face-safe mobile/desktop crops. Use a purpose-built non-Transform-branded observatory graphic for OG/social metadata; verify real previews. Do not use Transform branding or Steve Rolles imagery without permission.

## 13. Business model and stakeholder value

The best business value is a bespoke public-source monitoring prototype demonstrating taxonomy and synthesis. It should not be sold as current intelligence until update operations and QA are staffed. Define audience decisions, update SLA, analyst/reviewer roles, correction process, data licensing, alert delivery, accessibility and paid/private boundary. Engage Transform only with an accurate independent-demo framing.
