# Stage A recertification dossier — Entheogen Atlas

Audit date: August 13, 2026 HST  
Mode: full 17-section read-only recertification

## 1. Asset identity

Canonical <https://entheogen-atlas.vercel.app/>; private repository; one 436 KB client-side atlas. Disposition **P — retain one controlled research-atlas concept; high-risk tools and currentness claims release-blocked**.

## 2. Executive verdict

This is a substantial multidisciplinary information-design artifact with unusually broad taxonomy and numerous interactive views. It is also unsafe to present as a current, primary-source-validated medical/legal reference. Embedded data contains likely legal/clinical errors, mechanistic overclaiming, unvalidated scoring, culturally sensitive assertions and actionable dose/interaction material. Keep as a controlled research prototype until expert review, sourcing, currentness and safety governance are complete.

## 3. Complete route map

Only `/` is a route; internal “pages” are JavaScript states/modals. Privacy, terms, sitemap, robots and tracking endpoints are missing. Full inventory: [ROUTE_CONTROL_LEDGER.md](./ROUTE_CONTROL_LEDGER.md).

## 4. Complete control inventory

Navigation/search, ten substance modals, timelines, filters, radar/matrices, research/trial/legal/cultural views, glossary, RFRA issue-spotter and dose/interaction/risk calculators were source-inspected. No consequential action exists or was taken.

## 5. Consumer journeys

Researchers can browse rich synthesis but cannot consistently trace claims to exact sources. Clinicians/users may mistake dose/interaction outputs for safe personalized guidance. Legal/religious organizations may mistake heuristic RFRA analysis for current counsel. Indigenous readers encounter community knowledge reframed without visible authorship/permission/review.

## 6. Functional matrix

Static search/filter/chart/calculation works by embedded scripts; live research/legal/trial updating, validated decision support, downloads/API/accounts and professional review are absent. Analytics fails at 404.

## 7. Copy and information architecture

Separate fact, hypothesis, tradition/community account, legal status, editorial argument and tool output. Replace universal/categorical slogans. Add claim-level citations, date/jurisdiction, reviewer, uncertainty, correction history and source type.

## 8. Science and clinical evidence

Re-review every pharmacology, receptor, neural-correlate, clinical-trial, efficacy, risk and interaction claim with qualified clinicians/pharmacologists. Remove causal “neural basis” language where evidence is correlational. Validate no numerical phenomenology or safety score absent documented methodology.

## 9. Medical and harm-reduction boundary

Retire public dose calculators until independently validated and clinically governed. Do not recommend consumption. Add emergency/poison-control and high-risk contraindication pathways, medication/MAOI/cardiac/psychiatric/pregnancy/minor boundaries and explicit limits of remote information.

## 10. Legal and policy boundary

Revalidate every case, statute, exemption, schedule and jurisdiction against primary current authority. RFRA output is issue spotting only, never probability/advice. Correct district/circuit, RLUIPA/RFRA and ongoing-docket errors; identify federal versus state law and effective date.

## 11. Indigenous, religious and cultural governance

Use community-preferred names, distinguish archaeological inference from living knowledge, obtain community review/permission, respect data sovereignty and benefit sharing, avoid homogenizing “entheogen” categories, and address peyote conservation/appropriation. Do not score living traditions as substances/features.

## 12. Privacy, security and accessibility

Publish privacy/terms/editorial/corrections policies; minimize search/analytics data. Preserve hardening but remove unsafe eval where possible. Convert clickable divs to semantic controls, fix malformed nav attributes, restore focus, announce dynamic results, provide chart alternatives, non-color risk states and test keyboard/screen-reader/mobile/zoom.

## 13. Brand, portrait and authorship

Authorship is clear but credentials/scope need verification and independent reviewers. No portrait exists. Portrait is optional; if added, exactly one in the current About right column with verified local asset, rights/source, descriptive alt, intrinsic dimensions, optimized face-safe mobile/desktop crop. Never use it to imply clinical/legal authority. Replace broken wrong-domain OG image with an atlas-specific graphic and verify social previews.

## 14. Business and audience value

Best value is research architecture and long-form editorial synthesis, not decision support. Define whether audience is scholarly/editorial or clinical/legal; current attempt to serve all creates liability and maintenance burden. Sustainable release requires domain editors, source maintenance and corrections.

## 15. Ranked defects and release gates

Critical: actionable unvalidated dose/interaction/legal-risk tools; likely legal/clinical errors; false “all primary sources” claim. High: no live currentness, professional/community review, provenance or policy pages; mechanistic certainty; inaccessible controls; broken OG. Release gates: disable/label high-risk calculators, complete qualified review, claim-level sourcing/versioning, legal currentness, cultural governance, privacy/terms, accessibility testing and correction workflow.
